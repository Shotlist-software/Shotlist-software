# **🎬 Studiovity Shot List & Storyboard AI**

Plan. Visualize. Execute — From Script to Screen.

Studiovity is an **AI-powered shot listing and storyboarding software** designed for filmmakers, directors, and production teams to streamline pre-production workflows.

Transform your script into a **detailed visual blueprint** with intelligent shot division, AI-generated storyboards, and seamless team collaboration.

🔗 **Explore Product:** [https://studiovity.com/shotlist-storyboard/](https://studiovity.com/shotlist-storyboard/?utm_source=chatgpt.com)

---

## **🚀 Features**

### **🎥 AI Shot List Generation**

* Automatically convert scripts into structured shot lists  
* Define camera angles, movements, lens, and lighting  
* Customize **30+ parameters per shot**

### **🖼️ AI Storyboard Creation**

* Instantly generate visual storyboards from scripts  
* Maintain **character consistency across scenes**  
* Choose styles: Cinematic, Sketch, Anime, Cartoon

### **🎬 Script-to-Visual Workflow**

* Link shots directly to script scenes  
* Break down scenes into precise shot divisions  
* Ensure zero miscommunication on set

### **📱 Mobile \+ On-Set Access**

* Available on **Web, iOS & Android**  
* Edit and access shot lists in real-time during shoots

### **🤝 Collaboration & Export**

* Export shot lists & storyboards as **PDF / Excel**  
* Share with your entire production team  
* Real-time collaboration & asset management

### **🔗 Workflow Integration**

* Integrates with tools like:  
  * Final Draft  
  * Celtx  
  * Slack  
  * Google Calendar
